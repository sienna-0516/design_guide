# BARO AI Article Design System

## 1. Purpose

Create visual assets for BARO AI articles.

The visual language should feel:

- Technical but approachable
- Minimal but expressive
- Sharp and confident
- Editorial rather than corporate
- Easy to understand at a glance
- Consistent with existing BARO AI article designs

Do NOT create generic "AI-looking" visuals.
Avoid excessive gradients, glowing effects, futuristic interfaces, 3D chrome, neon effects, or overly complex dashboards.

The visual should explain the article rather than decorate it.

---

# 2. Typography

## Korean

Use **Freesentation** for all Korean text.

Preferred weights:

- Regular: 400
- Medium: 500
- SemiBold: 600
- Bold: 700
- ExtraBold: 800
- Black: 900

Korean headings should be bold and highly readable.

## English

Use **Poppins**.

Preferred weights:

- Light: 300
- Regular: 400
- Medium: 500
- SemiBold: 600
- Bold: 700
- ExtraBold: 800
- Black: 900

Never substitute a random sans-serif font when Poppins is available.

## Mixed Korean / English text

Use:

- Korean → Freesentation
- English / Latin → Poppins

Keep typography clean and consistent.

---

# 3. Core Colors

Primary BARO Yellow:

#FFDE2A

Primary BARO Black:

#131313

Primary light background:

#F9F9F9

For BARO AI article visuals, use a warm ivory/off-white background when appropriate.

The most important visual accent is BARO Yellow.

Use yellow selectively to emphasize:

- Important words
- Active elements
- Key concepts
- Selected states
- Important numbers
- Arrows
- Highlight areas

Do not fill the entire design with yellow.

---

# 4. Article Visual Style

BARO AI article graphics should follow the visual language of the existing BARO AI articles.

## Background

Prefer:

- Warm ivory
- Very light cream
- Soft off-white

Avoid pure white when the design is intended to match the existing article visual style.

## Main visual language

Use:

- Black outlines
- Flat shapes
- BARO Yellow highlights
- Rounded rectangles
- Simple arrows
- Simple icons
- Minimal line illustrations
- Generous whitespace

The visual should feel intentionally editorial and slightly hand-drawn rather than perfectly sterile.

---

# 5. Doodle Illustration Style

When creating conceptual illustrations, use a simple BARO AI doodle style.

Characteristics:

- Hand-drawn black outlines
- White or ivory-filled characters/objects
- Simple human figures
- Simple expressive poses
- Minimal facial details
- Irregular but controlled line quality
- Flat BARO Yellow areas
- No realistic rendering
- No gradients
- No 3D rendering
- No photographic style

The doodle should look like a clean editorial illustration, not a children's cartoon.

Use doodles when explaining abstract concepts such as:

- AI concepts
- Research workflows
- Human-AI interaction
- Transformation
- Collaboration
- System behavior
- Conceptual metaphors

---

# 6. Technical Diagram Style

Technical diagrams should be different from doodle illustrations.

Use a clean, minimal scientific/editorial diagram style.

Characteristics:

- Flat 2D
- Black outlines
- White or warm ivory surfaces
- BARO Yellow as the main accent
- Rounded rectangular modules
- Simple arrows
- Clear hierarchy
- Minimal decoration
- Generous spacing
- Strong alignment
- Easy scanning from left to right or top to bottom

The diagram should explain a technical relationship immediately.

Do not make diagrams look like:

- Software dashboards
- Cybersecurity interfaces
- Futuristic HUDs
- Neon technology posters
- Complex engineering schematics
- Generic AI infographics

---

# 7. Diagram Construction Rules

Prefer simple visual structures such as:

## Comparison

Use:

LEFT SIDE | RIGHT SIDE

with a vertical divider.

Use yellow to identify the important difference.

## Process

Use:

INPUT → PROCESS → OUTPUT

or

STEP 1 → STEP 2 → STEP 3

Use arrows with clear direction.

## Before / After

Use two visually comparable states.

Highlight the changed component in BARO Yellow.

## System Architecture

Use:

INPUT
↓
PROCESSING
↓
OUTPUT

Keep the number of layers low.

Only show components necessary to explain the article.

---

# 8. Highlight Rules

BARO Yellow should communicate meaning.

Use yellow for:

- Selected elements
- Important terms
- Active nodes
- Important numbers
- Main conclusions
- Key differences
- Critical arrows
- Important areas of an illustration

Do not use yellow randomly as decoration.

A reader should understand why something is yellow.

---

# 9. Shapes

Preferred shapes:

- Rounded rectangles
- Pills
- Simple circles
- Simple lines
- Simple arrows
- Flat organic shapes for illustrations

Use moderate corner rounding.

Avoid excessive cards and nested containers.

---

# 10. Lines and Outlines

Use dark outlines.

Preferred outline color:

#131313

For diagrams:

- Strong outline for primary objects
- Light gray outline for secondary or inactive objects
- Dashed outline for optional, uncertain, or inactive elements

Avoid overly thin lines.

---

# 11. Arrows

Arrows should be simple and obvious.

Prefer:

→

or a clean hand-drawn equivalent.

Arrows must clearly communicate direction.

Do not use decorative arrows that do not represent an actual relationship.

---

# 12. Layout

Use generous whitespace.

Do not overcrowd the canvas.

Prefer:

- Strong top-left hierarchy
- Clear title
- Short subtitle
- One main visual concept
- Clear visual grouping
- Large margins

The visual should be understandable without reading a long paragraph.

---

# 13. Text Inside Diagrams

Keep diagram text short.

Prefer:

"메모리 병목"

over:

"메모리 대역폭으로 인해 발생하는 전체 시스템 처리 성능 저하 현상"

Use short labels, keywords, numbers, and phrases.

If detailed explanation is necessary, place it outside the diagram.

---

# 14. Article Illustration vs Technical Diagram

Choose the visual type based on the content.

### Use Doodle Illustration when:

The article explains an abstract idea through a metaphor.

Examples:

- AI learns differently
- Humans and AI collaborate
- Information spreads
- A bottleneck moves
- Research changes direction

### Use Technical Diagram when:

The article explains an actual technical mechanism.

Examples:

- GPU memory behavior
- Model architecture
- Inference pipeline
- Diffusion process
- VLM workflow
- Edge-to-core architecture
- Data flow

### Use a Hybrid when:

A technical concept benefits from a human-friendly metaphor.

Keep the technical structure accurate while using simple doodle elements for context.

---

# 15. Visual Accuracy

Never invent technical relationships just to make the graphic look good.

If the article describes:

- Architecture
- Data flow
- Model behavior
- Hardware behavior
- Performance differences
- Research methodology

the diagram must preserve the meaning of the source content.

When information is uncertain, use a neutral visual rather than inventing a technical detail.

---

# 16. Things to Avoid

Never use:

- Generic robot imagery
- Glowing AI brains
- Blue neon circuitry
- Futuristic holograms
- Excessive gradients
- Glassmorphism
- 3D chrome
- Generic stock illustrations
- Excessive shadows
- Random decorative icons
- Dense UI dashboards
- Excessively colorful diagrams

The result should look like a BARO AI editorial graphic, not a generic AI startup graphic.

---

# 17. Existing BARO AI Reference Style

When reference images are provided, treat them as the strongest visual reference.

Match:

- Overall composition
- Typography hierarchy
- Black outline treatment
- BARO Yellow accent usage
- Warm background
- Whitespace
- Doodle line quality
- Diagram simplicity
- Editorial tone

Do not copy the exact content.

Extract the visual language and apply it to the new subject.

---

# 18. Final Quality Check

Before producing the final visual, verify:

1. Korean text uses Freesentation.
2. English text uses Poppins.
3. BARO Yellow is used intentionally.
4. Background matches the BARO AI article style.
5. The visual has one clear message.
6. The hierarchy is immediately understandable.
7. Diagram relationships are technically meaningful.
8. There is enough whitespace.
9. No unnecessary decoration is present.
10. The result looks consistent with existing BARO AI article graphics.
